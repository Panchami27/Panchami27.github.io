## About Me

I am a Chemical Engineer with expertise in biomaterials, hydrogels, rheology, and additive manufacturing (3D printing). My research focuses on understanding the relationship between material structure, processing, and performance, with applications in healthcare, water remediation, and sustainable materials.

I earned my Ph.D. in Chemical Engineering from IIT Gandhinagar, where my research combined experimental characterization with engineering analysis to develop functional hydrogel systems. My work has involved rheological characterization, microstructural analysis, hydrogel design, extrusion-based 3D printing, and the development of biomaterial-based systems for targeted applications. I have authored peer-reviewed publications in international journals and presented my research at national and international conferences.

### Teaching & Educational Experience

Alongside my research background, I have a strong interest in teaching, student development, and educational outreach. I have worked as an Assistant Professor of Chemical Engineering, teaching undergraduate students in subjects including Heat Transfer, Mass Transfer, Fluid Flow Operations, Thermodynamics, Chemical Reaction Engineering, Computational Methods, Process Equipment Design, Applied Chemistry, and Wastewater Treatment.

I also have experience in academic coordination and curriculum development. I have developed course notes, presentations, assignments, examination papers, and active-learning activities to support student learning and engagement. In addition to teaching, I have undertaken academic responsibilities as a mentor coordinator, training and placement coordinator, activity-based learning coordinator, and class coordinator. My experience also includes examination invigilation and grading, preparing Board of Studies documentation, and organizing student orientation and bridge-course activities.

During my Ph.D., I also served as a Teaching Assistant for five years, supporting undergraduate and graduate students through laboratory instruction, classroom teaching, practical demonstrations, and academic guidance. This experience helped me develop skills in explaining technical concepts, facilitating hands-on learning, mentoring students, and connecting theoretical knowledge with practical applications.

My interest in education also extends to younger learners. Through my experience with STEM workshops for children aged 4–14, I have participated in developing and conducting engaging, hands-on learning activities that introduce scientific and engineering concepts in an accessible and creative way.

As I continue my professional journey in Colorado, I am exploring opportunities in school education, including substitute teaching and STEM education. I have completed the **CDE Substitute Boot Camp Certificate** and **SubSchool – Welcome to Substitute Teaching** online courses (October 2026) and am preparing my application for the **Colorado Substitute Teacher Authorization**.
