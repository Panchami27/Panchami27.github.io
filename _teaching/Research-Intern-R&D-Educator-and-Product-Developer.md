Served as a research intern and educator to develop scientific kits and experiments and conducting STEM workshops for school children aged 4-14 

## Workshops conducted
- Concept of electricity and making LED light photo frames, LED light torch, LED bulb cards
- Making of a personal fan, learning working of circuits in series and parallel
- Acid-base reactions and making of turmeric and baking soda festival cards
- Chemistry concepts and making of slime and giant bubbles
- Concept of time and making your own clock
- Fun craft activities for small children aged 4-6

## Responsibilities included:
- Product development and testing
- Managing classroom
- Explaining STEM concepts and resolving any doubts
